Kids Planet – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026  |  Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short: Kids Planet does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information from children or parents. The app works offline. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kids Planet mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") treats information. The App is a learning and play app for young children (ages 3–5), available in English and Hindi, and is provided by Rakesh Singh.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses;
  • account or login details (there is no registration);
  • location data;
  • photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, or files from your device;
  • advertising identifiers or device identifiers for tracking;
  • any information through chat, social features or public profiles (the App has none).

2. Information stored on your device only

To work properly, the App saves some information only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to any server:

  • learning progress, completed activities, stars and badges;
  • app settings such as language (English or Hindi), sound and volume;
  • drawings and coloring pages saved by the child in the Creative Studio.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Children's privacy

Kids Planet is designed for young children. We follow the Google Play Families Policy. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not need to ask children for any data. Features that are meant for adults, such as settings, purchases and external links, are placed behind a parent gate. The parent gate is a simple check intended to keep young children out of adult areas. It is not an account-level security measure.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us (see section 9) and we will act on it promptly.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If premium features or subscriptions are offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details, name or billing address. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play of whether a purchase or subscription is active, so that premium content can be unlocked.

5. Advertising and analytics

The App does not show advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

6. Third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with third parties, because we do not collect any. The only third-party service involved is Google Play (for downloading the App and for purchases, as described above).

7. Permissions

The App does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permissions to read your personal files. An internet connection, where used, is only for Google Play purchases and subscription checks. All learning content is built into the App and works offline.

8. Data security

Because data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (for example, screen lock). We do not run servers that store information about you or your child.

9. Contact us

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App treats information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If we ever introduce collection of personal information, we will update this policy first and obtain any required parental consent before collecting it.
